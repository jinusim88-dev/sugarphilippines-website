Biscom Raw Sugar Supplier Philippines: Buyer's Guide to Negros Raw Sugar

A complete buyer's guide to Biscom raw sugar — the mill behind the brand, technical specs, pricing, and how to order wholesale.

Published: March 16, 2026 7 min read By: SugarPhilippines Team

If you're a food manufacturer, processor, or distributor looking for a Biscom raw sugar supplier in the Philippines, this guide covers everything you need to know — from the mill behind the brand to the technical specs and how to order wholesale.

Biscom raw sugar is a staple in the Philippine sugar supply chain. Produced in the heart of Negros Occidental — the country's sugar capital — it's a cost-effective feedstock for manufacturers, a natural ingredient for traditional Filipino products, and a reliable option for businesses that need unrefined sugar at scale.

What Is Biscom Raw Sugar?

Biscom raw sugar is produced by Biscom Inc. (Binalbagan-Isabela Sugar Company), a sugar mill located in Barangay San Vicente, Binalbagan, Negros Occidental — approximately 66 kilometers south of Bacolod City.

The municipality of Binalbagan is the oldest town on Negros Island, established in 1572, and has deep roots in the Philippine sugar industry. The BISCOM mill serves a milling district that covers Binalbagan, Himamaylan City, Hinigaran, Moises Padilla, and Isabela in Negros Occidental.

Raw sugar is the first product of the sugarcane milling process. After the cane is crushed and the juice is extracted, it goes through clarification, evaporation, and crystallization — but stops short of refining. The result is golden-brown sugar crystals that retain natural molasses, giving raw sugar its distinctive color, aroma, and flavor.

Unlike refined or washed sugar, raw sugar hasn't been processed to remove impurities and color. This makes it more affordable and well-suited for applications where it will be further processed or where a natural character is desired.

Why Choose Biscom Raw Sugar?

Biscom raw sugar is popular with commercial buyers for several practical reasons:

  • Lowest cost per kilo — raw sugar is the most affordable sugar type, making it ideal for high-volume manufacturing where refined color and purity aren't required.
  • Natural molasses content — retains the natural molasses from sugarcane, which adds color, moisture, and a mild caramel flavor useful in certain food products.
  • Feedstock for re-processing — many manufacturers and refineries purchase raw sugar as a feedstock to produce their own refined or specialty sugar products.
  • Reliable Negros supply — sourced from one of the largest sugar-producing regions in the Philippines, with consistent availability during and after milling season.
  • PRC-certified quality — quality analysis certified by a PRC-registered chemist (Allan A. Bello, RCh) using ICUMSA GS methods.
Key Takeaway

Biscom raw sugar is the right choice if you're a food manufacturer, industrial processor, or distributor who needs affordable sugar in bulk — especially for products like muscovado, kakanin, sauces, syrups, animal feed, or as feedstock for further refining.

Who Should Buy Biscom Raw Sugar?

Food Manufacturers & Processors

If you produce sauces, condiments, canned goods, or processed foods at scale, Biscom raw sugar offers significant cost savings. The natural color and molasses content are often masked or complemented by other ingredients in the final product.

Muscovado & Specialty Sugar Producers

Businesses that produce muscovado, organic-style sugar, or artisanal brown sugar products often start with raw sugar as their base material. Biscom's consistent polarization makes it a reliable starting point.

Kakanin & Traditional Filipino Products

Products like bibingka, puto, sapin-sapin, suman, and other traditional desserts pair naturally with raw sugar. The molasses character adds depth and authenticity that refined sugar can't replicate.

Syrup & Beverage Manufacturers

If you produce caramel syrups, molasses-based products, or flavored beverages where a brown sugar character is desired, raw sugar is the most cost-effective starting material.

Distributors & Resellers

Raw sugar has strong demand in wet markets, provincial retail, and among small-scale food producers. Distributors benefit from Biscom's consistent supply and competitive bulk pricing.

Industrial & Animal Feed Users

Some industrial applications and animal feed formulations use raw sugar as a carbohydrate source. The lower price point makes it economically viable at scale.

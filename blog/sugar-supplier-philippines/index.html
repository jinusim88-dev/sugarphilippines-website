How Pricing Works

Sugar pricing depends on three things: type, volume, and timing.

By type: Raw is cheapest (₱2,100–₱2,700/sack), washed is mid-range (₱2,400–₱2,900), and refined costs more (₱2,800–₱3,800). Choose the type that matches your product's needs — don't pay for refined if washed will work.

By volume: Larger orders get better per-sack rates. We offer volume pricing tiers from 10 sacks up to full truckloads. Recurring monthly orders get preferential pricing.

By timing: Prices are lowest during milling season (November–May) when supply peaks. Off-season (June–October) prices typically rise. Stock up during milling season if you have warehouse capacity.

Who We Serve

We supply sugar to a wide range of Philippine businesses:

Bakeries

From neighborhood panaderias making pan de sal to mid-size commercial operations producing pastries, cakes, and ensaymada. Most bakeries use washed sugar for everyday breads and refined for premium products.

Food manufacturers

Sauce, condiment, snack, beverage, canned goods, and processed food producers. Sugar type depends on the product and whether color matters.

Restaurants and commissaries

Central kitchens serving restaurant chains, fast food outlets, hotels, and catering companies. Need scheduled deliveries on a reliable timetable.

Distributors

Wholesale buyers who resell to wet markets, sari-sari stores, and provincial retailers. Typically buy raw or washed sugar in volume.

Beverage companies

Bottling plants, milk tea chains, juice bars, and coffee shops. Need refined or ICUMSA 45 sugar that dissolves cleanly.

Export-oriented food companies

Manufacturers in industrial parks and PEZA zones who produce for international markets and need certified sugar with proper documentation.

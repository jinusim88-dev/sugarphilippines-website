Quick guide — which type do you need?

  • If you're a bakery making pan de sal, ensaymada, or everyday breads → Washed sugar (saves ₱400–₱600/sack vs refined)
  • If you're a bakery making white cakes, icing, or pastries → Standard refined
  • If you're a food manufacturer making sauces, condiments, or canned goods → Raw sugar (cheapest option, color masked by other ingredients)
  • If you're a beverage company or produce clear drinks/syrups → Standard refined or ICUMSA 45
  • If you're producing for export or need Halal/FSSC certifications → ICUMSA 45 (Mitrphol)

Why "Near Me" Matters Less Than You Think

When searching for a sugar supplier, proximity is less important than delivery capability. Here's why:

Sugar is heavy.

A single 50kg sack weighs... 50 kilograms. A pallet weighs over a metric ton. You're not picking this up in your car. You need a supplier who delivers — and that means their delivery network matters more than their warehouse address.

Pricing doesn't vary much by location.

Whether you're in Makati or Malolos, Taguig or Taytay, the wholesale price per sack is essentially the same within our delivery zone. The bigger factor in your sugar cost is order volume and sugar type — not distance.

What actually matters:

  • Do they deliver to your exact address? (We cover all of Metro Manila + 7 provinces)
  • How fast? (48–72 hours standard)
  • Do they have your sugar type in stock? (We carry all 5 types)
  • Can they handle your volume? (10 sacks to truckloads)
  • Do they respond quickly? (Same-day quotes)
Key Takeaway

A supplier 5 km away who takes a week to respond and only carries one sugar type is worse than a supplier 30 km away who responds same day, carries everything, and delivers in 48 hours.

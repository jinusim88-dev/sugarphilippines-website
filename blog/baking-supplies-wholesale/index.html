Wholesale Buying Tips for Bakery Owners

  1. Don't use refined sugar for everything. This is the most common mistake. Washed sugar at ₱2,400–₱2,900 works for 60–80% of bakery products. Refined at ₱2,800–₱3,500 is only needed for white products. Switching saves ₱400–₱600 per sack.
  2. Track your monthly usage by product line. Know how many sacks go to bread vs cakes vs pastries. This lets you order the right mix of washed and refined — maximizing savings.
  3. Order on a schedule, not in emergencies. Running out of sugar mid-week and panic-buying at retail prices kills your margins. Set up recurring monthly deliveries to avoid this.
  4. Buy during milling season (November–May). Sugar prices are lowest when mills are producing. If you have storage space, buy a few extra sacks during this period.
  5. Consolidate your sugar order. One order of 40 sacks (mixed washed + refined) gets better volume pricing than two separate orders of 20.
  6. Negotiate for recurring volume. If you're buying 30+ sacks/month consistently, ask your supplier about preferential pricing for recurring orders. Reliable monthly volume is valuable to suppliers — use that leverage.

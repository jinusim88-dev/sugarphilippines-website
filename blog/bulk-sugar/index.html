Which Bulk Sugar Do You Need?

I run a bakery.

Washed sugar (Passi) for breads and everyday baking. Refined (Busco Standard) for white cakes and icing. Most bakeries order both.

I'm a food manufacturer.

Raw sugar (Biscom) if color doesn't matter in your product. Refined (Busco Standard) if it does. ICUMSA 45 (Mitrphol) for export or pharma.

I operate a restaurant or commissary.

Washed sugar for cooking and general use. Refined for desserts and beverages.

I'm a distributor.

Raw sugar for wet market and provincial retail. Washed sugar for bakery clients.

I produce beverages.

Refined (Busco Standard) minimum. ICUMSA 45 (Mitrphol) for premium or export beverages.

I produce for export.

ICUMSA 45 (Mitrphol) with Halal, Non-GMO, and Allergen-free certifications. COA provided.
